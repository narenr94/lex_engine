## 1. The Board Layout (The Data Array)

The Monopoly board is essentially a circular array of exactly **40 spaces** (indices `0` to `39`). A player's position is just an integer that updates via `position = (position + dice_roll) % 40`.

## 2. Core Game Rules & Engine State Logic

To make the engine work seamlessly, you need to enforce strict state management. Here are the core rules translated into logical requirements:

### Turn Flow & Dice Logic

* **The Roll:** Roll two 6-sided dice.
* **Doubles:** If a player rolls doubles (e.g., 4 and 4), they take their turn, resolve it, and **get another turn**.
* **Speeding Penalty:** If a player rolls doubles **three times in a single turn**, your engine must immediately move them to space `10` (Jail) and end their turn.

### Property & Rent Mechanics

* **Unowned Spaces:** When a player lands on an unowned property, they can buy it for the listed price. *Official Rule Note:* If they decline to buy it, it goes to auction among all players (though for a family game engine, you can skip auctions initially to keep it simple!).
* **Monopoly Multiplier:** If a player owns *all* properties of a single color group, the rent for any **unimproved** (no houses) property in that group is **doubled**.
* **Housing Rules:**
* Players can only buy houses if they own the complete color monopoly.
* You must build **evenly**. A player cannot build a second house on a property until all properties in that color group have one house.
* Maximum 4 houses per property. The 5th upgrade becomes a Hotel.



### Rent Calculation Math

Your engine will need a function to calculate dynamic rent based on current state:

* **Railroads:** Rent depends on how many railroads the owner has: 1 = $25, 2 = $50, 3 = $100, 4 = $200.
* **Utilities:** If the owner has 1 utility, rent is $4 \times \text{dice\_roll}$. If they own both, it's $10 \times \text{dice\_roll}$.

### The Jail State

If a player lands on "Go to Jail" (30), rolls 3 doubles, or draws a Jail card:

* They move instantly to index 10. They do *not* collect $200 if they pass GO.
* On their next turn, they have three choices to get out:
1. Pay a $50 fine before rolling.
2. Use a "Get Out of Jail Free" card.
3. Roll the dice. If they roll doubles, they get out for free and move that distance.


* If they fail to roll doubles by their **third turn in jail**, they are forced to pay $50 and must move forward using the final roll.

### Mortgages & Bankruptcy

* **Mortgaging:** A player in financial trouble can mortgage a property to get 50% of its purchase price back from the bank. Mortgaged properties **collect zero rent**. To unmortgage, they must pay back the principle plus 10% interest.
* **Bankruptcy:** If a player owes more money to another player or the bank than they can raise by selling houses and mortgaging properties, they must declare bankruptcy. They are eliminated, and all their remaining assets transfer to the creditor.
